🎓 What makes a strong password?

            
    
                
  • Length is the single biggest factor. Each extra character multiplies the search space.
    • 
                
  • Complexity uses uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols - bigger character pool = more entropy.
    • 
                
  • Uniqueness means avoiding dictionary words, names and common patterns attackers try first.
    • 
                
  • Entropy (bits) = log2(pool_size) × length. Every 10 bits roughly multiplies cracking time ~1000×.
    • 
                
  • Use a passphrase of random words - memorable yet strong.
